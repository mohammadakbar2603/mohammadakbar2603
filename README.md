I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at **Kansas State University**, advised by Dr. Doina Caragea, and a Graduate Research Assistant on an NSF SaTC 2.0 project. I build computer vision for hospital and home bedsides: models that watch infant patients continuously, from scarce and privacy-sensitive video, and still hold up on patients they have never seen.

**Looking for a Summer 2027 research internship** in computer vision or machine learning.

### What I'm working on

- **Infant distress recognition.** Adapting a facial-behavior model to infant faces and fusing it with infant facial action units or landmark geometry. 0.91 weighted F1 on 19 unseen patients at a children's hospital, up from 0.73 for the unadapted model. *(IEEE ICTAI 2026, first author)*
- **Tracheostomy-tube tracking (ongoing).** Fully automatic, real-time tracking of an infant's tracheostomy tube with Meta's SAM 2/3 and DINOv2, to flag accidental dislodgement.
- **3D bedside reconstruction (ongoing).** Periodic 3D reconstruction of the infant and surrounding objects with SAM 3D, toward complete bedside awareness.
